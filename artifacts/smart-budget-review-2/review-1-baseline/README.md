## Problem Statement
Customers shopping with a fixed budget often struggle to purchase essential items because of increasing prices, different brands, quantities, and changing product costs.

## Project Goal
The goal of this project is to develop a simple Smart Budget Shopping Assistant that helps customers:

- Track their available shopping budget
- Calculate their total expenditure
- View the remaining balance
- Compare lower-cost alternatives
- Make better purchasing decisions

## Background
This project continues from the previous AI Immersion activity. The initial study observed customers facing difficulty managing their shopping within a fixed budget.

## Proposed Solution
A simple digital shopping assistant will allow users to enter their budget, product price, and quantity. The system will calculate the total cost and remaining budget and help users make budget-friendly shopping decisions.
