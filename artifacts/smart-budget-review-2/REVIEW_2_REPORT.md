# Review 2 Report

## Project

Smart Budget Shopping Assistant — Project Better Tomorrow, C29 Semester 3.

## Problem

Customers shopping with a fixed budget find it difficult to manage essential purchases when prices vary or rise. They need a clear way to see total spending, remaining budget, and possible lower-cost alternatives while keeping their own priorities visible.

## Pathway

**Pathway A – Continuation Track.** Empathize and Define evidence is carried forward from Review 1. Review 2 concentrates on Ideate → Prototype → Test → Iterate.

## Review 1 baseline

Review 1 scored **30.8 / 35 (88%)** and established the problem statement, initial AI ideation, and a working one-product budget calculator.

## Review 2 development

The calculator was expanded into a responsive local-first workspace with shopping-list context, category budgets, Need vs Want intelligence, price comparison, recommendations, scenarios, analytics, goals, an assistant reflection flow, and evidence pages.

## AI usage

The AI audit documents the carried-forward Review 1 interaction and the Review 2 development decisions. The product does not claim a live LLM integration. Its intelligent behaviour is rule-based and explainable.

## Prototype features

- Dynamic dashboard calculations and budget-health score.
- Add/delete/mark-purchased shopping items.
- Category and Need vs Want summaries.
- Prototype-only price comparison.
- Explainable recommendation cards.
- What-if scenario controls.
- Current-list analytics.
- Savings goal tracking.
- Rule-based Ask SmartBudget prompts.
- Validation and evidence capture surfaces.

## Validation methodology

The student should run at least three real sessions using the prototype, give each tester a defined task, and record actual observations and scores in the Prototype Validation page and `USER_VALIDATION.md`.

## Iteration methodology

For each real issue: record the tester, feedback, problem, priority, improvement, before state, after state, status, and retest result. Only mark an item implemented or retested after the change actually exists and the flow has been checked again.

## Impact

Prototype-calculated outcomes are shown separately from validated user outcomes. The prototype can calculate plan totals, remaining budget, potential avoidable Want spending, and goal progress. Validated outcomes remain unavailable until real testers provide evidence.

## Limitations

The app has no accounts, cloud sync, live price API, historical dataset, or external LLM connection. Demo numbers are not research findings.

## Future work

Collect three genuine tester records, convert observed friction into iteration records, retest the changed flows, and then update the evidence report with the actual results.
