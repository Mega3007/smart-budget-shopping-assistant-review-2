# AI Ideation Audit

## Project Better Tomorrow – C29 Semester 3

### Project
Smart Budget Shopping Assistant

### Purpose of AI Ideation
AI is used as a divergence partner to generate multiple possible solutions, examine their strengths and weaknesses, and refine the most practical solution for the identified shopping-budget problem.

## AI Interaction 1 – Generating Possible Solutions

### Prompt Given to AI
Suggest practical solutions to help customers who have a fixed shopping budget manage their purchases when product prices increase or vary between brands.

### AI Suggestions
1. Smart budget calculator
2. Cheaper-brand comparison system
3. Shopping list with automatic budget tracking
4. Price alert system
5. Alternative product recommendation system
6. Budget progress indicator
7. Essential-versus-optional item classification
8. Simple mobile shopping assistant

### Decision
**Adopted and combined:** Smart budget calculator, cheaper-brand comparison, and alternative product recommendation.

### Reason
These ideas directly address the problems observed during the previous AI Immersion activity and can be combined into a simple prototype.

### Ideas Rejected
**Price alert system** was not selected for the first prototype because it would require continuously updated product-price data.

### Human Correction / AI Limitation
AI initially suggested features that would require real-time store databases. Since such data is not currently available, the solution was simplified to use prices entered by the user. This keeps the prototype realistic and testable.

## Selected Idea

The selected solution is a **Smart Budget Shopping Assistant** that allows a customer to enter a shopping budget, product price, and quantity. It calculates expenditure and remaining budget and can suggest lower-cost alternatives.
