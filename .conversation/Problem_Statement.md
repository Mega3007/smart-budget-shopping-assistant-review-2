## Background

During the previous AI Immersion activity, customers were observed while shopping within a fixed budget. The observations showed that customers often checked price tags, compared different brands, selected smaller quantities, chose cheaper alternatives, and sometimes removed items because of price increases.

## Identified Problem

Customers with a fixed shopping budget find it difficult to manage essential purchases when product prices vary or increase. They need a simple way to understand their total spending, remaining budget, and possible lower-cost alternatives while shopping.

## Defined Problem Statement

**How might we help customers shopping with a fixed budget make better purchasing decisions by tracking their expenses and identifying budget-friendly alternatives?**

## Target Users

Customers who need to purchase essential items within a limited or fixed shopping budget.

## Project Objective

To develop a simple Smart Budget Shopping Assistant that helps users track expenditure, calculate their remaining budget, and make more informed purchasing decisions.
